## Multi-account product catalog

PLAY_BLUE_PRODUCT_IDS is a comma-separated allowlist of additional, already provisioned Console subscription product IDs. The original PLAY_BLUE_PRODUCT_ID remains supported. Every additional product requires identical regional regular prices, a monthly auto-renewing base plan, and developer-determined firstmonth90 offer. This is a proposed finite catalog design, not verified approval or unlimited account support. No additional products are enabled by default.

The client fetches the configured catalog and actual Google Play purchase inventory. It restores any verified purchase owned by the signed-in Glint account, skips products whose receipts the server identifies as belonging to other Glint accounts, and selects the first unowned product for new checkout. Another account never inherits an existing receipt. Unknown verification failures and pending payments block checkout. No replacement or consumption of recurring subscriptions is used. The final checkout is checked against refreshed inventory and displayed offer; a changed SKU/offer requires another deliberate tap. Receipt verification and acknowledgement use Google's returned allowlisted product ID, rather than always acknowledging the primary product.

Capacity is the number of provisioned products per Google Play account. Exhaustion fails closed and must be disclosed; this is NOT arbitrary/unlimited concurrent subscriptions. First-discount history remains per Glint account across the entire catalog. Validate first/second/third account purchases on the SAME Play account and payment method in internal testing, independent cancellation and renewal, returning-account normal price, catalog exhaustion and cross-account restore. Google Play product approval, regional prices, offers and native device behavior remain unverified.
